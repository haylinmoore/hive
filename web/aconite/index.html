IRR

A route object for your ASN.

route:    a.b.c.0/24
origin:   ASyour-asn
source:   ARIN

RPKI

A signed ROA for your ASN.

prefix:     a.b.c.0/24
max-length: /24
origin:     ASyour-asn

Reverse DNS

The reverse zone, delegated to your nameservers.

zone:  c.b.a.in-addr.arpa
NS     ns1.your-domain
